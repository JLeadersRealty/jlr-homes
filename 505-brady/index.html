The Story

Open the door and see the whole thing.

In a lot of small towns, "new" means new paint. This one went up from a bare lot. The frame, roof, siding, windows, plumbing, electrical, heating & cooling, and the kitchen were all put in fresh for this house.

Walk in and the main floor opens straight back: living room, dining, then a kitchen with seating at the peninsula. It's all on one level, and nothing's been patched, painted over or called "good enough for now."

Picture your morning coffee on the covered front porch, looking out over Minden to the fields beyond.

The Primary Suite

Wake up to the field.

The primary bedroom is 13'2" x 21'6", with two windows. One of them looks out toward the farm fields at the edge of town. Through the hallway you'll find a 9'8" x 7'2" walk-in closet and a bath with a double vanity & a walk-in shower.

"Brand new inside. Wide open outside."
