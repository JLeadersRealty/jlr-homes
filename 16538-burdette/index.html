The Story

The deck is the spot.

Some houses have one place everybody drifts to. Here, it's the deck. It's covered by a pergola, right off the dining area, and it works for morning coffee, dinner outside or a long summer night by the grill.

Down the steps, the backyard is fenced, with raised garden beds, open grass and a little storage shed that has its own front porch.

The Main Level

One floor for the everyday.

Vaulted ceilings open up the living room. The kitchen's been updated in white, with a breakfast-bar peninsula, LVP floors and a pantry, and the dining area sits right next to it with a slider out to the deck.

All three bedrooms and both baths are on this same level, including a primary bedroom with its own three-quarter bath.

The Lower Level

A fireplace for movie night.

Downstairs, the family room has new carpet and a fireplace. It's a second place to spread out when the main floor's busy.

Past it, there's laundry and an unfinished area for storage, plus the 2-car garage with an opener and extra storage space.
