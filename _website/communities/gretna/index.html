Gretna in 60 Seconds

Gretna in 60 seconds

Gretna is a Sarpy County city on the southwest edge of the Omaha metro, with Highway 6 as its main route. It started in 1886, when the Burlington Railroad built a line between Omaha and Ashland and bypassed the old trading post of Forest City. The town was incorporated in 1889 and named for Gretna Green in Scotland. The 2020 Census counted 5,083 people, and the city now lists 9,054. The historic downtown runs along McKenna Avenue, where City Hall is. Gretna Public Schools serves the area. Nearby you'll find Nebraska Crossing outlets, Schramm Park on the Platte River, Holy Family Shrine and Vala's Pumpkin Patch. Every summer, Gretna Days fills the town with parades, a carnival and fireworks.

Jamie's Insider Take

What we love about Gretna

Gretna has an old downtown, newer parks and quick access to the rest of the metro. Here's what stands out.

McKenna Avenue downtown

The original downtown still runs along McKenna Avenue. City Hall is there, and the Saturday farmers market sets up downtown from June into October.

Gretna Days

This is the big one every late July. It has a grand parade, a carnival, a tractor pull, a car show, a music fest and fireworks.

Gretna Crossing Park

The park has ballfields, soccer fields, a dog park, an amphitheater for summer music, and the YMCA's outdoor aquatic center with slides and a lazy river.

Forest City Recreation Area

The Papio NRD built this 65-acre park around a 15-acre lake, west of 204th Street, with fishing and a playground. It's named for the trading post that came before Gretna.

Schramm Park and the Platte

Schramm Education Center sits on the Platte River. It has native fish exhibits, ponds, nature trails and a historic fish hatchery. Chalco Hills and Platte River State Park are also in the area.

Easy metro access

Gretna sits at the southwest edge of Omaha along Highways 6 and 370, with I-80 close by. Elkhorn, West Omaha, Papillion and Lincoln are all reachable for a daily commute.

Gretna FAQ

Questions about living in Gretna, Nebraska

What county is Gretna, Nebraska in?

Gretna is in Sarpy County, on the southwest edge of the Omaha metro area.

What school district serves Gretna?

Gretna Public Schools serves the city. The district has two high schools along with its middle and elementary schools.

How long is the commute from Gretna to Omaha?

One local guide puts the drive at about 10 to 20 minutes to Elkhorn or West Omaha and 25 to 45 minutes to downtown Omaha, depending on where you start. Highway 6 is the main route, and I-80 is nearby.

When is Gretna Days?

Gretna Days runs for several days in late July. The schedule has included a grand parade, a carnival, a car show and fireworks. Check gretnadays.com for this year's dates.

How did Gretna get started?

The town grew up in 1886 after the Burlington Railroad built a line between Omaha and Ashland and bypassed the trading post of Forest City. Gretna was incorporated on July 10, 1889.

What's the ZIP code for Gretna, NE?
