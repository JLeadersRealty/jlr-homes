Silver City in 60 Seconds

Silver City in 60 seconds

Silver City is a small town in Mills County, Iowa, with 245 people as of the 2020 Census. It got its start in 1879, when the Wabash, St. Louis and Pacific Railway was built through the area. Back in its busiest days, the town had a lumber yard, a grocery store, a jewelry store, a pharmacy and even a horse racing track. The old rail line is now the Wabash Trace Nature Trail, which crosses town on its 63-mile run between Council Bluffs and Blanchard. Silver Creek, the town's namesake, runs along the east edge of town. Students attend the Glenwood Community School District, and Mills County is part of the Omaha–Council Bluffs metro area. The ZIP code is 51571.

Jamie's Insider Take

What we love about Silver City

It's a small spot on the map, but there's more going on here than you'd guess.

The Wabash Trace runs right through

The old Wabash rail line is now the Wabash Trace Nature Trail, and it crosses Silver City from west to southeast. You can ride it north toward Council Bluffs or south toward Malvern, Shenandoah and Blanchard.

A trail-town pit stop

Riders on the Trace know Silver City as a place to stop. There's a shop in town that sells basic bike parts and provisions and lets you do your own repairs.

Real railroad history

The town started in 1879 when the Wabash, St. Louis and Pacific Railway came through. At one point it had a lumber yard, a grocery, a pharmacy, a jewelry store and a horse racing track.

Silver Creek

The town is named for Silver Creek, a tributary of the West Nishnabotna River that runs along the east side of town.

A library and historical society

For a town this size, having its own library and historical society says a lot about how much folks care about the place and its story.

Part of the metro, still small

Mills County is part of the Omaha–Council Bluffs metro area, and Glenwood, the county seat, is the school district hub.
