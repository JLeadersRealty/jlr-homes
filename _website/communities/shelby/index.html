Shelby in 60 Seconds

Railroad town. Interstate access.

Shelby is a town of about 730 people in western Iowa, sitting on the line between Shelby and Pottawattamie counties just off I-80. It grew up around the railroad in the late 1860s, was platted in 1870 and incorporated in 1877. Shelby kids attend the AHSTW Community School District. The town is known for its brick downtown, the Stone Arch Nature Trail that runs from I-80 through town, a park with new pickleball courts, and Carstens Farm Days every September. The city offers forgivable loans of up to $22,500 for building or buying new, and $5,000 for buying an existing home. J. Leaders Realty is based out of Shelby.

Jamie's Insider Take

What we love about Shelby

We don't just sell here. Shelby is where J. Leaders Realty is based, so these are our neighbors.

Right off I-80

Shelby sits just off the interstate, so getting to Council Bluffs, Omaha or anywhere east is easy. You get small-town quiet without being out in the middle of nowhere.

A real brick Main Street

Downtown still looks like a railroad town should: brick storefronts, the 1894 Masonic Hall and a general store on the corner.

A trail right through town

The Stone Arch Nature Trail runs from the interstate, through Shelby and well past town. It's named for the old Rock Island stone arch viaduct, which is on the National Register of Historic Places.

Carstens Farm Days

Every September the Carstens 1880 Farmstead fills up with antique tractors, steam threshing and a tractor parade. It's one of the best farm-history weekends around.

Shelby Park & pickleball

The town park sits right on the trail, with new pickleball courts, a big playground, shelters with grills and a ball diamond for summer softball and Legion baseball.

Incentives to buy or build

The City of Shelby offers forgivable loans of up to $22,500 to build or buy new, $5,000 to buy an existing home, and 0% rehab loans. See the programs.

City of Shelby Housing Programs

Shelby will help you buy, build or fix up

The City of Shelby runs four housing programs. Here's the short version. The fine print is in the city's guidelines.

Build or buy new: up to $22,500

Households earning $200,000 or less can get a five-year forgivable loan of 10% of the purchase cost, up to $22,500, when they build or buy a brand-new home. It's forgiven as you live there as your primary home. Builders selling to qualifying buyers can also get a credit of 2.5%, up to $3,500.

Buy an existing home: $5,000

A five-year forgivable loan of $5,000 toward buying an existing home in Shelby. It's for households at or below 120% of the county median income for their family size who haven't recently owned a higher-value home.

Fix it up: up to $10,000 at 0%

Owner-occupied homes can get a no-interest rehab loan of up to $10,000 over five years with a 20% match. Qualifying rentals can get up to $5,000 with a 50% match. The rehab loan can be combined with the existing-home program.

Tear down an eyesore

The city helps property owners demolish dilapidated buildings, and can make grants to nonprofits that buy and fix up or remove them.

Apply before you start

The City Council approves every application, and nothing can be approved once construction has started. Loans need an approved bank, and the forgivable loans are secured by a mortgage until they're satisfied.

We'll walk you through it

We know these programs and can help you line up the house, the lender and the application. Questions for the city go to Shelby City Hall at 712-544-2404.

Shelby FAQ

Questions about living in Shelby, Iowa

Where is Shelby, Iowa?

Shelby is a town of about 730 people in western Iowa, on the line between Shelby and Pottawattamie counties, right off I-80. Harlan, Avoca and Minden are nearby, and Council Bluffs and Omaha are an easy drive west.

What school district is Shelby in?

Shelby is in the AHSTW Community School District. Country homes outside town can fall into other districts, so we always check the specific address.

Why do people move to Shelby?

Buyers like the small-town feel, the price range and how close it is to I-80. Our Shelby sales have ranged from in-town homes around $50,000 to country homes around $500,000.

Does Shelby offer incentives for buying or building a home?

Yes. The City of Shelby offers a five-year forgivable loan of 10% of the purchase cost, up to $22,500, for households earning $200,000 or less who build or buy a new home; a $5,000 forgivable loan for buying an existing home (income limits apply); and no-interest rehab loans of up to $10,000. Apply before construction starts. Terms can change, so confirm with Shelby City Hall at 712-544-2404.

What is there to do in Shelby?

Carstens Farm Days every September is the big one, with antique tractors and steam threshing. Shelby also has new pickleball courts at Shelby Park, the Stone Arch Nature Trail running from I-80 through town, a Fourth of July hosted by Shelby Fire & Rescue and a brick downtown.
