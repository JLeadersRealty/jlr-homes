Good to Know Before You Cross the River
The transfer tax is different on each side. Nebraska's documentary stamp tax went up to $3.32 per $1,000 of value in July 2026. Iowa's transfer tax is $1.60 per $1,000. On a $400,000 sale that's about $1,328 in Nebraska and about $639 in Iowa. We'll put the real number on your net sheet.
Moving from Omaha to an Iowa acreage? Rural Iowa homes with a septic system need a Time of Transfer inspection before closing, and most have a private well to test too. Our Western Iowa Acreage Guide walks you through it.

Omaha Metro FAQ

Questions about buying and selling in the Omaha metro

Can J. Leaders Realty sell my home in Omaha, Elkhorn or Papillion?

Yes. J. Leaders Realty is licensed in Nebraska (firm license 20230475) and Iowa (firm license F06561000), so we can list and sell homes anywhere in the Omaha metro, including Omaha, Elkhorn, Papillion, La Vista, Gretna and Bellevue, and help you buy on either side of the river.

What do I get when I list with J. Leaders Realty?

Every listing gets a full marketing campaign, its own property website with drone and twilight video, staging with our own furniture and décor at no extra cost, a hand-built pricing plan and seller net sheet, and weekly updates. That's the same for a $250,000 home or a $2.5 million estate.

Is Elkhorn part of Omaha?

Yes. The City of Omaha annexed Elkhorn in 2007, so Elkhorn addresses are inside Omaha city limits in Douglas County. Elkhorn Public Schools still serves much of the area.

What is the median home price in the Omaha metro?

The median closed price for existing homes in the Omaha metro was $330,000 in August 2026, up 8.2% from a year earlier, with 989 existing-home sales that month, according to Great Plains Regional MLS data reported by Grow Omaha. Prices vary a lot by neighborhood, so ask us for numbers on your area.

How much is the transfer tax when selling a home in Nebraska vs. Iowa?

Nebraska's documentary stamp tax is $3.32 per $1,000 of value as of July 18, 2026. Iowa's real estate transfer tax is $1.60 per $1,000 after the first $500. The seller usually pays it in both states. We'll include the exact amount on your seller net sheet.

Can you help me sell in Omaha and buy in Council Bluffs?

Yes, and it's one of the biggest reasons people call us. Because we're licensed in both states, one team can time your Omaha sale and your Iowa purchase together, whether that's a Council Bluffs neighborhood, a small town like Neola or Minden, or an acreage in the Loess Hills.
