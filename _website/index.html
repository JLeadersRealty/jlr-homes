How We Sell

Every listing gets a real campaign. Not just an MLS entry.

Price doesn't decide how hard we work. A $63,000 building on Main Street gets the same plan as an acreage.

Talk about selling
  1. Figure out who the buyer is

    Before a single photo, we decide who's most likely to buy your home and what they care about. Everything else is built around that.

  2. Media day

    Professional photos, drone, and video, shot from a plan we write for your home ahead of time.

  3. Your home gets its own website

    A dedicated property site with a day-to-twilight drone opener, the full photo story, and a direct line to the listing agent.

  4. A 12-piece content campaign

    Social posts, reels and email built around your home's story, scheduled from launch day through closing.

  5. Targeted ads and a weekly update

    Ads aimed at the right buyers, plus a weekly update from us so you're never wondering what's going on.

Questions

Straight answers

The questions we hear most. Don't see yours? Ask us. We'll give you a real answer, not a sales pitch.

What areas does J. Leaders Realty serve?

We help buyers and sellers across western Iowa and the Omaha metro, including Council Bluffs, Omaha, Minden, Neola, Avoca, Shelby, Harlan, Missouri Valley, Underwood, Treynor and Carter Lake, plus acreages and rural property throughout Pottawattamie, Harrison and Shelby counties.

Is J. Leaders Realty licensed in Iowa and Nebraska?

Yes. J. Leaders Realty is a licensed real estate firm in both states (Iowa firm license F06561000, Nebraska firm license 20230475), so we can help you on either side of the Missouri River.

Do you help buyers and sellers with acreages?

Yes, acreages are a big part of what we do. Recent sales include 18396 Jaylen Dr (1.01 acres) and 18580 Taylor Ln (1.23 acres) outside Council Bluffs. We know the questions that come with country property, like wells, septic systems, outbuildings and how to price land that doesn't have many comparable sales.

How does J. Leaders Realty market a home for sale?

Every listing gets professional photos, drone and video, its own property website, a 12-piece content campaign across social media and email, and targeted online ads. Sellers get a weekly update so they always know what's happening.

How much does it cost to sell my house?

Commissions are always negotiable, and your total cost depends on your price, your mortgage payoff and closing costs. Use the seller net sheet calculator on this page for a quick estimate, or ask us for a detailed net sheet with your real numbers.

How many clients has J. Leaders Realty helped?

Our team has closed more than 336 sales for buyers, sellers and investors, with over $70 million in total sales and 28 combined years of experience.

How do I contact J. Leaders Realty?
