The Story

Everything you use every day is on one level.

The kitchen, living room, primary bedroom, a second bedroom, two full baths and the laundry are all on the main floor. No stairs to get through a normal day.

The kitchen has granite countertops, a gas range and a peninsula that looks right into the dining area. From there, a sliding door takes you out to the deck.

It's on a quiet street in Malvern, close enough to Glenwood, Council Bluffs and Omaha for work, far enough to feel like a small town.

The Living Room

A fireplace with a view of the trees.

The living room is built around a brick gas fireplace, with a big window on each side. Both look out to the deck and the wooded backyard.

It's a good room for a cold January night. Flip on the fireplace and watch the snow come down on the yard.

"Flip on the fireplace and watch the snow."
The Primary Suite

Your own bath and a walk-in closet.

The primary bedroom has two windows facing the backyard and its own bathroom with a double vanity. Step past the vanity and there's a walk-in closet with double rods.

Downstairs, the finished lower level adds a family room with a second gas fireplace and a half bath, plus unfinished space for storage and a workbench.
