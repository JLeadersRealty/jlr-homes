The Story

The hard part's already handled.

Most small-town commercial buildings come with a to-do list before you can even open the door. This one doesn't. The HVAC, flooring, windows, doors, electrical and plumbing were all redone in 2016, and the roof, water heater and fiber internet were added in 2024.

It's 444 square feet on Main Street. It was most recently a beauty salon, and it's small enough to keep your overhead low. If you've been waiting for the right spot to start something, this is a pretty easy yes to look at.

The Space

Simple, clean & ready to work.

Inside, there's an open main room with wood-look floors, a drop ceiling and big windows that pull in a lot of light. Off of it you've got a restroom with a grab bar and a utility room with laundry and a utility sink. The concrete block exterior and a newer roof keep the building side of things easy too.

Office, studio, salon, a little shop: picture what fits your business, then check with the City of Minden that it's a permitted use.
